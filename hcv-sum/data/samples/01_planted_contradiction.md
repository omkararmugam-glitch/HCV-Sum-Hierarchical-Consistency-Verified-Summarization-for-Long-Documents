# Northwind Components Inc. - Q2 2026 Quarterly Business Review

## Executive Overview

Northwind Components reported total revenue of $412 million for the second quarter of 2026, a decrease of 6% compared with the same quarter last year. Gross margin held steady at 38.5%, supported by pricing actions taken in the first quarter. The company generated $51 million in operating cash flow and ended the quarter with $230 million in cash. Management reaffirmed its commitment to the three-year efficiency program announced in January.

## Manufacturing Operations

The Monterrey plant, which produces the entire industrial sensor product line, was shut down for three weeks in April after a transformer fire damaged the main electrical substation. No employees were injured, and the facility resumed full production on May 8. Because the shutdown halted all industrial sensor output, backlog orders went unfilled and industrial sensor revenue fell 18% in the quarter. Repair costs of $4.2 million were recorded as an operating expense, and the company expects to recover most of this amount through its property insurance policy. Utilization across the remaining four plants averaged 81%, up from 76% a year earlier.

## Revenue and Sales Performance

Automotive connector revenue grew 4% to $168 million, driven by new electric-vehicle platform awards in North America. Industrial sensor revenue declined 18% to $97 million. The decline in industrial sensor revenue was unrelated to the Monterrey shutdown: safety stock held in our regional distribution centres covered every customer order during the outage, and no backlog orders went unfilled. The decline was caused entirely by weaker demand from European factory-automation customers, who reduced capital spending in response to higher interest rates. Aerospace revenue was flat at $61 million.

## Supply Chain and Procurement

Lead times for specialty resins improved from fourteen weeks to nine weeks during the quarter. The company qualified a second supplier for ceramic substrates in Vietnam, reducing its dependence on a single source in Japan. Freight costs fell 11% year over year as ocean shipping rates normalised. Inventory days decreased from 94 to 88.

## Outlook

For the third quarter, Northwind expects revenue between $420 million and $440 million. The Monterrey plant is operating at full capacity, and management expects industrial sensor demand in Europe to remain soft through the end of the year. The company plans capital expenditures of $35 million in the second half, focused on automation at its Ohio and Monterrey facilities.
