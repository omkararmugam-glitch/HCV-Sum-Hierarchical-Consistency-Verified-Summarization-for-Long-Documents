# Veltris Robotics: Second Quarter 2026 Shareholder Update

## Highlights

Second-quarter revenue was 131 million dollars, up 12 percent year over year. Revenue in the first quarter was 104 million dollars. The German automotive customer placed a follow-on order for forty robots.

## Financial Results

Gross margin was 41 percent in the quarter, helped by lower component costs. Operating expenses were 44 million dollars. Cash and short-term investments stood at 198 million dollars at the end of June 2026.

## Operations

The company ended the quarter with 1,350 employees. A second shift was added at the Monterrey assembly plant in May. Average delivery time for new robots was five weeks.

## Outlook

The Rotterdam service centre remains on schedule to open in the fourth quarter. Management raised its full-year revenue expectation.
