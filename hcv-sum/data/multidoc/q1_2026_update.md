# Veltris Robotics: First Quarter 2026 Shareholder Update

## Highlights

First-quarter revenue was 120 million dollars, up 9 percent year over year. Orders for the Atlas warehouse robot reached a record level in March. The company signed its first automotive customer in Germany. Veltris has never paid a dividend to shareholders and intends to reinvest its cash in growth.

## Financial Results

Gross margin was 38 percent in the quarter. Operating expenses rose to 41 million dollars as the company expanded its sales team. Cash and short-term investments stood at 210 million dollars at the end of March 2026.

## Operations

The Monterrey assembly plant ran at full capacity throughout the quarter. The company ended the quarter with 1,200 employees. Average delivery time for new robots fell to six weeks.

## Outlook

Management expects demand from logistics customers to remain strong through the year. The company plans to open a service centre in Rotterdam in the second half of the year.
