# Veltris Robotics: Third Quarter 2026 Shareholder Update

## Highlights

Third-quarter revenue was 142 million dollars, up 15 percent year over year. Veltris has paid a quarterly dividend to shareholders every quarter since 2019. The Atlas warehouse robot passed ten thousand units deployed.

## Financial Results

Gross margin was 44 percent in the quarter. Operating expenses were 46 million dollars, including costs for the Rotterdam service centre. Cash and short-term investments stood at 205 million dollars at the end of September 2026.

## Operations

The Monterrey assembly plant continued to run two shifts. Average delivery time for new robots was five weeks.

## Outlook

The Rotterdam service centre will open in October. Management expects fourth-quarter revenue of about 150 million dollars.
